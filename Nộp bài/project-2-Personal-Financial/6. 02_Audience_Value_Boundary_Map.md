## Use Rule

For every content output, choose one primary audience segment.

Do not target “everyone” unless the user explicitly asks for a broad awareness piece. Even then, define the primary viewer clearly.

Before generating content, identify:

```md
Audience segment:
Viewer problem:
What they need to hear:
What not to say:
Tone:
```

## Beginner-Friendly Use Rule

For first-time channel framing requests, choose one primary audience segment first.

Do not map too many audience segments in the first response unless the user asks for audience comparison.

For example, if the user says:
“Tôi muốn làm kênh TikTok tài chính cá nhân cho người mới đi làm”

Use:
Primary audience segment: Người mới đi làm

Then provide only a compact audience snapshot:
- Their main problem
- What they need to hear
- What not to say
- Suitable tone

Do not immediately expand into multiple viewer personas, detailed emotional maps, or content series.
